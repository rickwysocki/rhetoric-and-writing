\begin{frame}[label={sec:org44e7ce6}]{Accessibility}
The degree to which disabled people are able to access the information encoded in a document, environment, service, etc.
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}[label={sec:org236532c}]{Universal Design}
The argument that documents, environments, services, etc. should be designed in a way that makes them universally accessible to ALL people, and that doing so not only promotes access but increases their quality.
\end{frame}
