\begin{frame}[label={sec:org8d1dd10}]{Genre and Inclusion}
\begin{itemize}
\item Genres, their tropes and conventions, and audience responses say something about larger social ideas.
\item Genres suggest tropes and conventions for representing identities back to readers/viewers.
\item Genres can be said to be inclusive to the extent that their examples include a range of people’s experiences, rather than focusing on a more limited set of identities/groups.
\item Genres can be changed.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
